Pub Classic Scoring App

9 Dart Century

Nine darts. Three turns. Hit exactly 100 or finish as close as possible without going over. Miss outside the scoring area and your score resets to zero. Bounce-outs score nothing and do not penalize.

Match Setup

Enter one player per line. Choose the board type before starting.

1
Goal: Score exactly 100, or the nearest total below 100 after 9 darts.
2
Bust: Go over 100 and the player is out of the game.
3
Miss: Outside scoring area resets that player’s score to 0.
4
Bounce-out: No penalty, no points. It still counts as a dart thrown.
5
Default hit: Every number is single unless you press double, triple, or quad first.

Scoring Guide

Bull counts as 25 on single and 50 on double. Triple bull and quad bull are disabled. Miss means outside the scoring area and resets the player’s running total to zero.

Ex. S20 = 20 Ex. D20 = 40 Ex. T20 = 60 Ex. Q20 = 80 MISS → 0 BOUNCE → 0 pts

Game State

Use this app as a clean scorer for pub matches and practice sessions.

A
Turns: Each player gets one 9-dart sequence in this app flow.
B
Undo: Removes the most recently entered dart before submit.
C
Submit: Advances to the next player only when pressed.
D
Result: Closest valid score to 100 wins. Exact 100 beats all.

Board Feel

Styled to evoke a classic dartboard palette: warm brass, dark timber, felt green, and chalkboard contrast. Fast input, large tap targets, and a readable scoreboard make it practical on desktop or mobile beside the oche.
